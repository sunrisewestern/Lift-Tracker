First public release of Lift Tracker.

**Downloads**
- `LiftTracker-1.0.0.apk`: the phone app (Android 8.0 or newer).
- `LiftTracker-Wear-1.0.0.apk`: the optional Wear OS watch app (Wear OS 3 or newer). Both are signed with the same key, which the watch link needs.

**What's in it**
- Log workouts set by set: warm-up, normal, failure and drop sets, weight & reps, bodyweight reps, duration or distance, with a rest timer, notifications and a live workout notification.
- Routines with folders, an editor, and one-tap start.
- An exercise library with pictures, how-to steps, per-exercise history, records and your own custom exercises.
- Profile dashboard with statistics, a monthly report, a training calendar and a muscle heat map.
- AI coach with your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible provider), plus AI help to fill in exercises and build or tune routines.
- Health Connect: workouts, heart rate and calories written out; body weight and body fat read in.
- Wear OS companion: start and follow workouts, complete sets, rest timer and heart rate from the watch.
- Import workout history (CSV) and routines; export to CSV or JSON; encrypted backups to a file or a WebDAV folder.
